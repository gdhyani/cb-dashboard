cb works with apps that run on Node.js today. Support for running your app on Deno is planned, but not available yet.

## Status

Deno isn't supported yet. cb's [preload](/docs/guides/develop/preload-and-api) — the part that routes your app's connections to protected services — is loaded by Node.js, and Deno doesn't load it. An app started with `deno` gets none of your cb values.

## What to do meanwhile

Run the app with Node.js. If your project uses npm packages and standard Web APIs, it often runs on Node.js with few or no changes. Once it does, follow [Node.js (Express)](/docs/platforms/node-express), [Fastify, Koa, Hono](/docs/platforms/node-frameworks) or your framework's page in [Supported platforms](/docs/platforms).
