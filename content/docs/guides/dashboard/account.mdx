title: Account preferences

**Account preferences** shows who you are signed in as, lets you pick the dashboard's theme and lists where your
account is signed in. Open it from the account menu (your initials, top right) or the person icon in the left rail.
Every member has this page, whatever their role.

## Profile

Your name and email, read-only. They can't be changed from the dashboard yet.

## Appearance

Choose **Dark**, **Light** or **System**. System follows your computer's setting and switches when it does. The
choice is kept in this browser and applies to the dashboard only; these docs stay dark. You can also switch from
**Theme** in the account menu, or from search (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>).

## Sessions

- **Dashboard** lists the browsers signed in to your account. **This browser** marks the one you are using. Choose
  **Revoke** on any other to sign it out; it has to sign in again. Owners and admins see this list; developers see a
  note, because only admins can list sessions.
- **CLI devices** lists the laptops where you ran `npx cb login`, with the agent status. **Revoke** ends that laptop's
  access at once. See [Revoke a device](/docs/guides/admin/revoke-device).

**Sign out** in the section header signs out this browser.

## Find your way around

| Where | What it does |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Your organization, the **project switcher** and, inside an environment, the **environment switcher**; search (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>), Docs and the account menu on the right |
| Left rail | Home, Projects, Members, Devices & Sessions, Activity, Emergency Stop, My Access, Account. It widens to show labels when you point at it; **Sidebar control** at the bottom keeps it **Expanded**, **Collapsed** or **Expand on hover** |
| Project menu | Inside a project: Overview, Access, Settings and the project's environments (tabs under the title on narrow screens) |
| Phones | The menu button at the top left opens the same list |
