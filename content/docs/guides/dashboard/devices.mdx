The **Devices & sessions** page (under **Security** in the sidebar) shows every laptop logged in with the CLI and
every browser signed in to the dashboard, across all members. Owners and admins see this page. Developers manage
their own devices on [My access](/docs/guides/dashboard/my-access).

<Frame src="/docs/images/guides/devices.png" alt="Devices & sessions page with CLI devices and Dashboard sessions" caption="Devices & sessions page with CLI devices and Dashboard sessions" />

## Devices and sessions are different things

A [device](/docs/overview/concepts#device-and-session) is a laptop that ran `npx cb login`. It is what runs your
app: its agent opens connections through the cb gateway. A session is a browser signed in to the dashboard. It
manages cb but never connects to your services.

| | **CLI devices** | **Dashboard sessions** |
|---|---|---|
| Created by | `npx cb login`, approved in the dashboard | Signing in to the dashboard |
| Shows | Device name, owner, OS, last seen, and **Agent online** (with open connections) or **Agent offline** | Browser and OS, email, last active; **This browser** marks your own |
| Action | **Revoke device** | **Sign out** |

## Revoke a device or sign out a session

| | Revoke device | Sign out (session) |
|---|---|---|
| What stops | The device's token and every open connection from it, immediately | That browser's dashboard sign-in |
| Running apps on that laptop | Lose their database and API connections; `cb run` reports the revocation | Not affected |
| Other devices of the same person | Not affected | Not affected |
| Getting back in | The person runs `npx cb login` again, which creates a new device | The person signs in again in that browser |
| Recorded in Activity | Yes | No |

Choose the action from the row's "…" menu and confirm. You can't sign out your own current browser from this page;
use **Log out** at the bottom of the sidebar instead.

<Callout type="tip" title="Lost or stolen laptop">
Revoke the device first. If you can't tell which device it is, or you want to block a person everywhere until you
know more, use an [emergency stop](/docs/guides/dashboard/emergency-stop) on **A person**. The step-by-step is in
[Revoke a device](/docs/guides/admin/revoke-device).
</Callout>
