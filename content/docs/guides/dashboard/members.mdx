The **Members** page lists everyone in your [organization](/docs/overview/concepts#organization) and lets owners and
admins invite new people. Inviting someone makes them a member; it doesn't give them access to any environment yet.

<Frame src="/docs/images/guides/members.png" alt="Members page with the Invite panel and the People list" caption="Members page with the Invite panel and the People list" />

## Invite someone

cb doesn't send email. You generate an invite link and share it yourself, for example in your team chat.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose a role

Under **Invite**, pick a **Role**: `developer`, `admin` or, if you are an owner, `owner`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Limit it to one address (optional)

Enter an **Email (optional)** to make sure only that address can accept. Leave it empty for **Anyone with the link**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Generate and copy the link

Select **Generate invite link**, then **Copy invite link**. The link works once and expires in 7 days.
</Step>
<Step>
### Share it

Send the link to the person. They open it, create an account or sign in, and join the organization.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Grant access next">
A new developer can't run anything until you grant access. See
[Grant and revoke access](/docs/guides/admin/access).
</Callout>

### Pending invites

Links that haven't been used yet appear under **Pending invites**, with the email (or **Anyone with the link**), the
role and when it expires. Choose **Revoke invite** in the "…" menu to stop a link from working.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Everything, including inviting and promoting owners. |
| `admin` | Manage projects, variables, access, members (except owners), devices, activity and emergency stops. |
| `developer` | Run the environments they are granted; see projects read-only; manage their own devices. |

To change a role, pick a new one in the person's role list. Admins can't change an owner's role or give the owner
role; only owners can. The full permission table is on [Projects and environments](/docs/guides/dashboard/projects#roles).

## See what someone can reach

Select a member's name, or choose **View access** in their "…" menu, to open a panel with everything that person can
reach, grouped by project. Use it when someone changes role or leaves. Owners and admins have access to every
project, so the panel says so instead of listing projects.

## Remove a member

Choose **Remove member** in the person's "…" menu. You can't remove yourself, and admins can't remove owners.

<Callout type="danger" title="Removing a member closes their connections">
The person loses access to every project in the organization, and their live connections are closed. To pause
someone without removing them, use an [emergency stop](/docs/guides/dashboard/emergency-stop) on **A person**
instead.
</Callout>
