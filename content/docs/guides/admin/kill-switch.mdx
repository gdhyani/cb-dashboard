---
title: Use the kill switch
description: Choose the right emergency stop for an incident, from one laptop to the whole organization.
---

The kill switch is the **Stop access** action on the [Emergency stop](/docs/guides/dashboard/emergency-stop) page.
It closes live connections within seconds and refuses new ones until you restore access, without deleting
anything. This guide helps you pick the narrowest scope that contains an incident. It is for owners and admins.

## Pick the scope

| Situation | Scope in **What to stop** |
|---|---|
| A laptop is lost or stolen and you know which one | **A device** |
| A person's account may be compromised, or they shouldn't connect until you've talked to them | **A person** |
| A database or API key may have leaked, or the service is misbehaving | **A resource** |
| Something is wrong in one environment, such as `staging` | **An environment** |
| You suspect a breach across the organization and can't narrow it down | **Everyone** |

<Callout type="tip" title="Start narrow">
Stop the smallest scope that contains the problem: a device before a person, a person before an environment. You
can always add a wider stop, and each stop is restored on its own.
</Callout>

## Stop access

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Emergency stop

In the sidebar, under **Security**, open **Emergency stop** and select **Stop access**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the scope and target

Pick the scope under **What to stop**. For **A resource**, choose the environment first, then the service.
</Step>
<Step>
### Type the reason

Enter a **Reason**, for example `Suspected credential leak`. It is required and is recorded in
[Activity](/docs/guides/dashboard/audit).
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm

Select **Stop access**. Within seconds, live connections close and developers in scope see
`cb: access revoked by admin`.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="danger" title="Everyone includes you">
**Everyone** stops the whole organization, admins and owners too. The dashboard keeps working, so you can restore
access from the Emergency stop page.
</Callout>

## After the stop

1. Find the cause in [Activity](/docs/guides/dashboard/audit): filter by **Usage** and **Devices**.
2. Fix it. Typical fixes are [revoking a device](/docs/guides/admin/revoke-device),
   [rotating a secret](/docs/guides/admin/rotate-secret) or
   [revoking someone's access](/docs/guides/admin/access#revoke-access).
3. Restore access: on the Emergency stop page, choose **Restore access** in the stop's "…" menu. Running apps
   reconnect automatically.

## Other ways to stop access

| Tool | Scope | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Suspend environment** (environment "…" menu) | One environment | You want the environment's own page to show it as **Suspended**, with a reason. Resume with **Resume environment**. |
| **Revoke device** | One device, for good | The laptop won't be used again. |
| **Remove access** | One person, one project or environment | Access should end, not pause. |
| **Remove member** | One person, the whole organization | Someone leaves. |
