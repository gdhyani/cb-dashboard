---
title: Revoke a device
description: Cut off one laptop immediately when it is lost, stolen or no longer used.
---

Every laptop that runs `npx cb login` becomes a [device](/docs/overview/concepts#device-and-session) with its own
token. Revoking a device stops that token and closes every connection from it at once, without touching the person's
other devices or their access. This guide is for owners and admins; developers can revoke their own devices from
[My access](/docs/guides/dashboard/my-access).

## When to revoke a device

- A laptop is lost or stolen.
- Someone replaced their laptop and the old one should stop working.
- A device you don't recognise appears in the list.

Nothing on a revoked laptop gives access to your real services: apps there only ever had stand-in values, and those
work only through the cb gateway, which now refuses them.

## Revoke it

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Devices & sessions

In the sidebar, under **Security**, open **Devices & sessions**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Find the device

Under **CLI devices**, find the device by its name, owner and OS. **Agent online** with a count of open connections
means it is in use right now.
</Step>
<Step>
### Revoke

Open the row's "…" menu, choose **Revoke device** and confirm.
</Step>
</Steps>

The token stops working immediately and every open connection from the device is closed. Apps there get
`cb: access revoked by admin`, and `cb run` refuses to start. The revocation is recorded in
[Activity](/docs/guides/dashboard/audit).

<Callout type="danger" title="Revoking can't be undone">
A revoked device can't be re-enabled. If the person still uses that laptop, they run `npx cb login` on it again and
approve the new device in the dashboard.
</Callout>

## Revoke device, sign out or emergency stop?

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| One known laptop is lost or retired | **Revoke device** |
| A browser is still signed in to the dashboard somewhere | **Sign out** under **Dashboard sessions** |
| You don't know which device is affected, or want a person blocked everywhere for now | [Emergency stop](/docs/guides/dashboard/emergency-stop) on **A person** |
| You want to pause one device and maybe allow it again later | Emergency stop on **A device**, then **Restore access** |
| Someone leaves the team | **Remove member** on [Members](/docs/guides/dashboard/members) |

<Callout type="tip" title="Secrets shown as-is">
If the environment has any **Secret shown as-is** variables, the real values were on that laptop. Replace them
after revoking. See [Rotate a secret](/docs/guides/admin/rotate-secret).
</Callout>
