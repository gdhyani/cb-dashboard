Everyone who takes part in cb — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. This page
summarises it; the full text is in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` in each repository.

## Our pledge

We want participation in cb to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect for differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory on the affected repository, or a
direct message to [@gdhyani](https://github.com/gdhyani) on GitHub. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the maintainers respect the privacy and security of the person reporting.

The maintainers may respond with a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on
the impact of the behaviour, as set out in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
